---
title: Death of Otakar Motejl, Czech Ombudsman
date: 2010-05-10T00:00:00.000Z
perex: With great regret we would like to announce the death of Mr. Otakar
  Motejl, Public Defender of Rights, who died suddenly after a short illness on
  Sunday 9 May afternoon in the University Hospital Brno-Bohunice.
categories:
  - news
---
Mr Motejl held the post since December 2000 when he was elected the first Czech ombudsman. He was re-elected in 2006.

Public opinion polls regularly showed him as one of the most trusted Czech officials.
